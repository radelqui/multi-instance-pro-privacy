Privacy Policy — Multi Instance Pro

Last updated: August 29, 2026

Multi Instance Pro ("the app"), published by RepackagerExpress.com (Carlos De La Torre, carlos@sypnose.cloud), is a desktop tool that manages isolated instances of applications already installed on your computer.

What we collect

Nothing. The app runs entirely on your local machine. It does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal data, telemetry, analytics, or usage information with the publisher or any third party.

Third-party software

The app manages existing installations of third-party applications (such as AI assistant desktop apps). Those applications have their own privacy policies that govern the data they process. This app never accesses, reads, or transmits the contents of your sessions or conversations.

API keys (optional feature)

If you configure the optional "AI Engine" feature with your own API keys, those keys are stored only on your local machine and are sent only to the AI provider endpoint you choose. The publisher never sees them.
